### 2. DETAILED BREAKDOWN OF WHAT THE CLIENT WANTS

#### Client Profile & Domain
* **Who is the client?** A professional designer, former user of Issuu, associated with NIFT and NID communities. 
* **Target Audience:** Design students, professionals, and peers from institutions like NIFT and NID.
* **Business/Content:** High-end design portfolios, fashion shows, detailed project archives, educational architecture/design projects, and monthly press/news clippings. He commands extensive distribution channels, managing ~100+ WhatsApp groups for NIFT and ~100+ for NID, driving thousands of views per project natively.

#### Problems with Issuu & Current Workflow
* **The Issuu Paywall Issue:** The client used to host ~100 detailed design project publications/magazines on Issuu for free, but Issuu transitioned to a paid model, demanding recurring fees he considers a waste of capital/investment.
* **Objective:** Wants to own his platform entirely (independent of third-party publishing subscriptions) with a custom domain, eliminating recurring platform ransom fees.

#### Exact Website & Feature Requirements
* **Core Platform Concept:** A custom replica of **Issuu.com (issuu.com / iww.com)** hosted independently.
* **Magazine / Flipbook Viewer:**
  * Interactive page-turn/swipe mechanism mimicking a physical magazine or Kindle reader (smooth animated page flips, NOT just standard scrolling).
  * Clean presentation of multi-page PDF documents grouped by categories (Fashion Shows, Detailed Projects, Educational Projects, Press/News clippings).
* **Content Management / Uploads:**
  * Ability to easily upload new monthly PDF magazines/news clippings, update, upgrade, or delete content dynamically.
  * Integration for displaying massive volumes of standalone JPEG/image assets structured similarly to **Pinterest (Masonry grid layout)** for his massive fashion and design image archives.
* **Lead Capture & Gating Mechanism:**
  * **Mandatory Visitor Registration/Gate:** Any visitor/student wanting to view his portfolio or magazines *must* submit their Name, Email ID, and Phone Number before accessing the content, feeding a proprietary lead generation database.
* **Blog Section:** A dedicated blog module to publish monthly design articles and updates.
* **Social & Community Integration:** Seamless connectivity to his social media profiles (LinkedIn, Instagram, etc.) and cross-platform sharing capabilities.

#### Design, UI, & Branding Preferences
* **Color Palette:** Simple, elegant color palette featuring **Indigo** tones.
* **Typography & Layout:** Clean, minimalist typography; clean navigation with 1 to 5 structured pages.
* **Aesthetic Vibe:** Professional, editorial, publication-grade layout matching elite design portfolios.

#### Business Goals, Traffic, & Monetization
* **Traffic Volume:** Proven track record of generating 148,000 to 250,000+ views per project on Issuu, totaling ~1 million monthly views via his NIFT/NID WhatsApp network (over 200+ active groups with hundreds of members each).
* **Monetization & Profit Sharing:** Open to discussing revenue/profit-sharing models if the agency can help scale monetization, provided upfront costs are realistic and not exorbitant.

#### Budget, Pricing, Domain, Hosting, & Timeline
* **Historical Cost Reference:** Spent ~₹15,000 to ₹20,000+ three years ago for his previous setup, plus domain/hosting fees.
* **Hosting / Domain:**
  * Has an existing `.org` domain that needs to be checked for renewal/reactivation; if expired/unavailable, a brand new domain will be purchased.
  * Needs adequate cloud storage space (estimated **10 GB initial cloud storage**) to handle massive image and PDF libraries.
* **Timeline:** No extreme rush, but expects a structured proposal and cost estimate shortly.

#### Client Nuances & Specific Questions
* Emphasized that he understands the tech and design space well, wants transparent pricing, and rejects inflated dev quotes (such as ₹2 Lakhs+ quotes which he considers wasteful).
* Requested clear deliverables regarding data security, backup, and smooth scalability.
