Scope of Work

1. Interactive 3D Flipbook Magazine Engine (Issuu Replacement)
  • Realistic animated page-turn & touch-swipe experience for multi-page PDF documents (Kindle / physical magazine feel)
  • Fully responsive design: optimized for smartphones (touch swipe), iPads/tablets (dual-page view), and desktops with high-res zoom
  • Categorization & Tagging: Fashion Shows, Retail Projects, Education & Workshops, Monthly Editions (Jan, Feb, etc.), and Press Clippings
  • Publication Manager: Easy-to-use admin dashboard to upload, update, categorize, or delete PDF magazine editions anytime
2. Gated Access & Automated Lead Generation System
  • Mandatory viewer registration wall requesting Full Name, Email ID, and WhatsApp / Phone Number before unlocking full magazine reading
  • Live Lead Capture & Sync: Captured visitor details are recorded securely and sync directly to an exportable dashboard / Google Sheet
  • Returning visitor recognition to avoid redundant re-prompting for verified users
3. Pinterest-Style Masonry Gallery & Monthly Editorial Blog
  • Dynamic masonry grid layout showcasing thousands of high-resolution JPEG runway, fashion show, and retail installation images
  • Full-screen lightbox zoom and high-speed media preview with progressive image loading
  • Dedicated Press, News & Monthly Blog module to publish design articles, newspaper clippings, and milestone achievements
4. Minimalist Luxury Indigo UI & High-Traffic Cloud Infrastructure
  • Bespoke luxury editorial aesthetic utilizing your requested Indigo Color Palette (Deep Indigo, Slate Blue, Crisp White, Slate Grays)
  • 5 Core Page Architecture: Home / Shelf, Magazine Catalog, Masonry Gallery, Press & Blog, About & Direct WhatsApp / Social Contact
  • High-traffic cloud storage (10 GB+) & Cloudflare CDN architecture engineered to effortlessly handle 100k–300k+ monthly viral views with near-zero recurring hosting costs
